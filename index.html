Summary

Recap

Chapter 9 of Robert Breaker’s The Unbroken Chain of Salvation by Faith in The Blood contains 87 AI-generated quotes out of 91, with the remaining four unverifiable.

He knows it contains AI-generated quotes and, despite his promise to pull it from Amazon, it is still up half a year later. Robert Breaker released his book, The Unbroken Chain of Salvation by Faith in The Blood : The Biblical Doctrine of Putting Your Faith in the Blood Atonement of Jesus Christ to Receive the Forgiveness of Sins to show that his salvation theology has historical backing.

His Salvation Theology

This is his salvation theology.

1. Salvation is by faith alone.

2. The Blood of Jesus Christ is the distinct object of saving faith.

3. Calling upon God as a condition for salvation (such as Dr. Ruckman taught) is faith + works.

This passage unmistakably shows that salvation is by FAITH and not of works, and that THE OBJECT OF OUR FAITH must be THE BLOOD OF JESUS, as it clearly says in verse 25 with the words: “Through faith in his blood.” - Robert Breaker, The Unbroken Chain of Salvation by Faith in The Blood, Chapter 4
"Clearly salvation is a heart thing. A person must TRUST the blood sacrifice of Christ with all his HEART to be saved. It’s what he trusts in from the heart (the blood of Christ) that saves him, not what he says from his MOUTH." - Robert Breaker, The Importance of the Blood, Chapter 2.
"Faith in the Bible is also spoken of as: believing in, trusting in, confiding in, resting in, and relying upon something. For us to be saved, we must believe in, trust in, confide in, rest in, and rely upon the blood of Jesus to take us to heaven. For FAITH IN THE BLOOD of Jesus is the only way to be saved." - Robert Breaker, The Unbroken Chain of Salvation by Faith in The Blood, Introduction

Robert Breaker believes you are not saved unless you explicitly and consciously trust the physical shed blood of Jesus Christ for salvation.

“He's looking at your heart, and he's looking to see: When does your heart trust the blood alone for salvation? You can come to him begging him, ‘Please don't send me to hell,’ but it's not until, from the heart, you're trusting in the blood that you're saved.” - Robert Breaker, The Message of the Blood
“You see, the confession isn't what saves you; it's the belief in the heart. So, if you're not saved and you want to be saved and you don't want to go to hell, why don't you just bow your head and close your eyes and just go to the Lord and tell him that what you've heard from the scripture today is true, because the Bible says it is. Now, why don't you say, ‘I accept what you've done for me. I'd rely upon that finished work. I trust your blood to save my soul’? See, the prayer isn't what saves you; it's whether or not your faith is in the shed blood of Jesus Christ. If your faith is not in that blood, then you're not saved. If your faith is in that blood, then you're on your way to heaven.” - Robert Breaker, How to Be Saved
“Is to confess with their mouth that the Lord is Jesus or Jesus is Lord. Wouldn’t that be Israel? So what I read Romans 10, I see Jews asking Paul, ‘Hey, what about Israel?’ And he says, ‘Well, if a guy is a Jew and he has confessed that Jesus is his Lord, that Jesus is his Messiah, and if he believes that God hath raised him from the dead, well, then he can be saved.’ So if he believes in who and what, then he’ll be saved. I don’t think he’s telling you you have to do both, and that you have to say with the mouth, or you’re not saved. If he’s saying you have to believe from your heart in something and say it with your mouth at the same time, then that would be a faith plus works gospel. And that would nullify what Jesus did, because it’s not just what he did; it’s what you say, which would be a work.”

The ChatGPT Pattern

In Chapter 8, Robert Breaker used ChatGPT to “research,” which resulted in 44 of the 78 quotes being AI-generated, 31 being unverifiable, and only 3 being genuine.

In Chapter 9, Robert Breaker also used ChatGPT to “research,” resulting in 87 out of 91 quotes being AI-generated and the remaining 4 being unverifiable because their citations come from obscure sources.

93.8% of the preachers spanning from 1954 to 2023 all speak in the exact same, identical three-part robotic formula:

“[Thing X] doesn't save you.” “Romans 3:25 says [verb] 'through faith in His blood'.” “That's not [X], that's [Y] / That's the gospel / It's the blood or hell!”

The third thing is specifically ChatGPT writing.

"You know where this is coming from, don’t you? “It’s not X, it’s Y” is an AI mainstay. It’s one of ChatGPT’s most insidious tells. No matter how innocuous a prompt you enter, AI will always find a way to sneak it into its response. Ask it if you should put more ham in your pasta, and it will tell you: “Ham doesn’t just taste good – it makes everything else taste better.” Ask it if you should chase a bee around your garden and it will say: “Bees aren’t stupid – they’re hyper-specialised”." - Stuart Heritage, ChatGPT’s latest stylistic quirk is sinister, infuriating – and absolutely everywhere, The Guardian
